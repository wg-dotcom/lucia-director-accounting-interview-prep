02 · The business

Know the leadership mandate behind the title.

The Company needs a senior accounting leader who can still work at transaction level while building the close, controls, system, and future team.

  • A healthcare technology, services, and data business focused on psychiatry.
  • Supports more than 800 independent psychiatry practices across 49 US states.
  • Combines clinical software, billing and revenue-cycle services, and life-sciences projects.
  • A San Francisco-based Series B Public Benefit Corporation launching a new platform.
  • Three entities and several revenue streams create real close, intercompany, allocation, and revenue-recognition complexity.

Close and reporting

Own month-end, quarter-end, and year-end close across GL, AP, AR, and payroll for three entities. Deliver accurate US GAAP financials on a reliable timeline.

Multi-entity accounting

Own intercompany transactions, allocations, entity reporting, and the parent-level rollup across the MSO structure.

Technical accounting

Research and document positions, especially ASC 606 across software, revenue-cycle billing, and life-sciences projects.

Transition in-house

Take accounting back from the outsourced provider. Capture knowledge, close documentation gaps, and protect continuity.

Controls and process

Build policies, close procedures, review standards, and clear decision rights.

Systems, automation, and AI

Move from QuickBooks Online Premium to a true multi-entity platform. Redesign the chart of accounts. Use AI where it improves control and speed.

Business partnership

Give Shuchi and leadership clean numbers, clear analysis, and recommendations before they need to ask.

Build first, manage second

Do the work personally and operate with minimal support. Build the team only when the workload justifies it.

06 · Pressure test

Answer the concerns directly.

Open each concern. Practice the answer, then replace every general statement with proof.

01Will you stay hands-on in a lean role?

The truth. Your most recent scope included about 30 accounting professionals and 250 clients. The Company may start with no accounting team.

Answer it like this

“Yes. I built the work before I governed it. I personally designed the workflows, US GAAP standards, controls, policies, migration methods, escalation reviews, fractional-CFO models, and AI training. I want another build-from-the-ground-up mandate, and I understand that the first phase requires me to do the detailed work.”

Bring this proof. Choose one close, migration, policy, or client escalation you personally executed from source data to final decision.

02Direct multi-entity consolidation needs clearer proof

The truth. Your screening covered portfolios, clients, business units, and large teams, but it did not clearly define legal entities, intercompany accounting, or consolidations.

Answer it like this

“I want to be precise about the difference between clients, business units, and legal entities. I will walk you through the exact entities I have closed, the intercompany work I owned, and what was consolidated. Where my experience is transferable rather than identical, I will say so.”

Bring this proof. Prepare the exact entity count, currencies, intercompany entries, eliminations, ownership structure, and final reporting package.

03NetSuite is not established

The truth. You described migrations into QuickBooks and between QuickBooks versions, not a QuickBooks-to-NetSuite implementation.

Answer it like this

“NetSuite is not the migration I covered in my screening. My direct experience includes requirements, data mapping, migrations into QuickBooks, validation, workflow design, training, and cutover control. I would apply that discipline while learning the target platform and would not make the system decision before understanding the entities, integrations, reporting, and control needs.”

Bring this proof. Review NetSuite multi-entity basics and prepare a platform-selection scorecard and migration plan.

04ASC 606 authorship needs proof

The truth. Healthcare pricing and Controller work are relevant, but the screening did not establish that you wrote ASC 606 accounting positions.

Answer it like this

“I will not equate pricing models with technical revenue-accounting authorship. I would assess each contract through the five-step model, document the performance obligations, variable consideration, allocation, and timing, and use technical review when the fact pattern requires it.”

Bring this proof. Bring one complex revenue judgment you did own and separate pricing analysis from accounting policy.

05Why did the Secure Box role end?

The truth. The screening described Secure Box as your most recent role but did not establish the timeline or reason for leaving.

Answer it like this

“I will give you a direct timeline and explain why the role ended without turning it into a long story. The important point is what I built, what I learned, and why I now want another directing controllership role where I can build from the ground up.”

Bring this proof. Prepare a factual two-sentence answer with dates, reason, and what you want next.

06Can your AI example withstand technical questions?

The truth. The AI story is differentiated, so Shuchi may test whether you owned the accounting design or are using the label loosely.

Answer it like this

“The product connected to QuickBooks and produced two outputs: CFO insights and accounting-compliance review. My responsibility was to define the accounting logic, train the system, test the output, and keep human judgment in the approval path. I can explain the inputs, failure risks, validation, and what the tool was not allowed to decide.”

Bring this proof. Prepare the workflow: data source, model task, prompt or rules, validation set, human reviewer, error handling, privacy controls, and measurable result.
